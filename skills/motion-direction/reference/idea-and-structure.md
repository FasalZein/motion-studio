# Idea and structure, any length

The same process makes a 15 s spot and a 3-minute film. Length adds units and a rhythm plan; it does not add a different method.

## Idea first

- Write one **idea line**: one sentence, no "and", that says what the film tells. It goes in `meta.logline`. [S: clair-simple, oddfellows]
- Pick one **central device** that carries the idea: a metaphor, a world or a camera logic. Every beat serves it. [S: if-jessica-jones, clair-boards]
- Ask of every beat: which part of the idea line does this beat tell? A beat with no answer is cut or merged. [I]

## Audio first

- Plan against the track, voice or a click track before any board. Cuts, camera hits and reveals land on the grid. [S: buck-2021, kurzgesagt, if-candy, flavien]
- The board phase writes the grid with `motion-studio beats`; see the `motion-studio` board brief. [D: D41]

## Directions, not palettes

- Offer 2 to 3 directions for G1. Each differs in idea device, world and camera logic, not only in color or type. [S: buck-2016, if-jessica-jones; D: D61]
- Each direction is shown as a moving look test with one seam that moves on both sides and one type or UI beat. [D: D61]
- Record the chosen direction and the reason. [S: buck-2016]

## Iterate the board, not the render

- Fix idea, order and seams on the board and the moving animatic, where a change is cheap. A render is the wrong place to discover the structure. [S: clair-boards (five board passes), buck-2021]
- The board sets the ambition; name a technique per shot that the engines render cheaply. [S: gunner]

## Longer films

For films over about 45 s, add these three plans to the board. All three are proposals until the e2e and tracer films measure them.

- **Sequence types.** Define a few reusable sequence types (for example hero reveal, list-to-diagram morph, camera fly-through, type moment) and vary them; avoid the same type twice in a row. [P, from kurzgesagt and gunner unit logic]
- **Pacing curve.** Write a density curve (fast or slow per sequence) with at least one breath and one peak. Mix the predictable and the surprising. [S: flavien ("roller coaster"); curve form P]
- **Motifs.** Pick 1 to 3 recurring motifs (a shape, a color move, a camera move) and place each in the opening, the middle and the end. [P]
- **Open loops.** Plant an unfinished element early, show it again at section boundaries, advance it visibly and close on the image you opened on. [S: video-storytelling]
