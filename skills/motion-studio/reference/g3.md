# G3 presentation

G3 asks the director to approve the motion: the moving animatic of the blocking builds on the real track. Show real artifacts from the film folder, in this order, in one message. Read every value from its file or the command output; quote no value from memory.

1. **Animatic.** `animatic.mp4` (H.264; it plays in QuickTime and browsers): the stitched blocking renders of the primary format with `audio.track` and, for a film with a voice, the narration from `voice.startFrame`. State: `Blocking at placeholder fidelity: timing, camera paths, seam threads and living holds move; finish comes in the full build.`
2. **Liveness advice.** The liveness verdict `motion-studio animatic` printed, copied in full: each limit and each still span with its shot and frames. Say which spans went back to their builders before this gate and which remain. The verdict is advice at G3; G4 enforces the limits.
3. **Approved board.** The `## Beat contract` table of `beatmap.md` with its `Thread` column, and the frozen G2 contact sheet `stills/approved/G2-<hash8>/<format>/sheet.png` of the primary format, so the director compares the motion with the approved poses and threads.
4. **Choices.** `approve`, `changes` with notes that name shots or beats, or `rescope`. State the note rounds used out of 3.

Before presenting, `motion-studio animatic films/<slug>` exits 0 on the current blocking renders (it refuses a shot render older than its source) and `motion-studio status films/<slug>` shows G2 approved, not stale.

Record the decision with `motion-studio gate films/<slug> G3 <approve|changes|rescope> --note "<director's words>"`, then `motion-studio taste notes films/<slug> G3` for a note. Apply notes as the skill's **Notes** section states. Completion: `motion-studio status films/<slug>` shows the recorded G3 decision; after an approval, `node <this skill>/evals/check.mjs g3 films/<slug>` prints `g3 ok`.
