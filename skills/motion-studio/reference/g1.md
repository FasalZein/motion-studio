# G1 presentation

G1 asks the director to choose one direction. Show real artifacts from the film folder, in this order, in one message. Read every value from its file; quote no value from memory.

1. **Brief.** The full `BRIEF.md`, not a summary, with its intake rounds and every `(default)` field.
2. **Hero assets.** One row per ledger entry used by any look test: ledger id, type, source URL and capture date, license status. The logo, one font file per family, the palette and at least one real capture appear here.
3. **Directions** (2 or 3). For each `<look-id>`:
   - idea device, world and camera logic, and look, copied from its `Direction <n>:` line;
   - the look's brand source (palette, type, radius, motif from the mark), or the `motion-look` file it falls back to and why;
   - its applied pattern replacements (`<template pattern> → <chosen move>`);
   - the clip `stills/G1/<look-id>.mkv` (FFV1; it plays in VLC or mpv) and the poster `stills/G1/<look-id>.png`;
   - the liveness report `stills/G1/<look-id>.liveness.json`: every `limits[]` row as metric, value, limit and pass; `longestStillSeconds`; `movingShare` beside `movingShareWholeFilm`.
4. **Style bible.** For each reference in `style-bible.md`, its `- Take:` and `- Do not take:` lines, copied. With no reference, say `No reference supplied`.
5. **Independence.** Any review of the look tests is `independent` (a fresh subagent or the user) or `non-independent` (this agent). State it beside the review.
6. **Choices.** `approve <look-id>`, `changes` with notes on the brief or a named look test, or `rescope`. State the note rounds used out of 3.

Record the decision as `motion-look` step 5 states: write `storyboard.json` `look` first, then `motion-studio gate films/<slug> G1 <approve|changes|rescope> --note "<director's words>"`, then `motion-studio taste g1`. Completion: `motion-studio status films/<slug>` shows the recorded G1 decision, and each direction shown has its clip, poster and a report whose every `limits[]` row passes.
