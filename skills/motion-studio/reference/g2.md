# G2 presentation

G2 asks the director to approve the beat map, its seam threads and the poses. Show real artifacts from the film folder, in this order, in one message. Read every value from its file; quote no value from memory.

1. **Beat contract.** The `## Beat contract` table of `beatmap.md` in full, every row and column, including `Thread`, `Spoken line` and `Sound cue`. Below it, the output of `motion-studio beatmap films/<slug>`: one row per shot with its thread, spoken line, reveals and sound cues. A `missing` thread there is a board defect: fix it before presenting.
2. **Engines.** The `## Engines` table of `beatmap.md`: engine, reason and UI source per shot. For each shot with `threeD`, its reason from `storyboard.json`. The `Hero shot:` line and whether its source was promoted from `shots/_look/<look-id>/` or rebuilt.
3. **Sourced assets.** The `## Sourced assets` table, or the `No brand material:` line.
4. **Beat grid.** From `storyboard.json` `audio`: `grid`, `bpm`, `confidence` and the counts of beat, downbeat and drop frames. With a voice, the `voice.wordTimings` file and `voice.startFrame`.
5. **Stills per beat.** For each beat row, its start still and its end still in the primary format: `stills/G2/<format>/<shot-id>-f<NNN>.png`, where `<format>` is `16x9`, `9x16` or `1x1` and NNN is the shot-local frame zero-padded to 3 digits (a beat `60-120` in a shot that starts at frame 60 shows `-f000.png` and `-f059.png`). Put the beat's motion event beside its pair.
6. **Seams.** For each seam, the outgoing beat's end still and the incoming beat's start still side by side, with the thread (`kind: shared`) between them.
7. **Contact sheets.** `stills/G2/<format>/sheet.png` for every chosen format, built by `motion-studio stills` from the stills G2 hashes.
8. **Scope.** State: `Stills approve poses and threads. Motion is judged at G3 on the moving animatic.`
9. **Choices.** `approve`, `changes` with notes that name beats or shots, or `rescope`. State the note rounds used out of 3.

Before presenting, `motion-studio validate films/<slug>` reports no error other than stale approvals (a stale G2 or later gate is expected while it is being presented again) and `node <this skill>/evals/check.mjs board films/<slug>` prints `board ok`.

Record the decision with `motion-studio gate films/<slug> G2 <approve|changes|rescope> --note "<director's words>"`, then `motion-studio taste notes films/<slug> G2` for a note. Apply notes as the skill's **Notes** section states. Completion: `motion-studio status films/<slug>` shows the recorded G2 decision; after an approval, `motion-studio status films/<slug>` shows `G2 approved` (it also catches a gate input added after approval) and `node <this skill>/evals/check.mjs g2 films/<slug>` prints `g2 ok`.
