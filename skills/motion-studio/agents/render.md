# Render and delivery phase

Inputs: approved storyboard and frozen stills, shot projects (blocking for G3, full afterwards), layouts, ledger, beat grid, audio source files and current gate hashes. Output: `animatic.mp4`, per-format draft and final bundles under `renders/<format>/`, mix and reports.

1. For G3, build the moving animatic from the blocking renders. After every blocking builder reports completion, run `motion-studio render films/<slug> <primary format>` and `motion-studio stitch films/<slug> <primary format>`, then `motion-studio animatic films/<slug>`. `animatic` needs an approved G2. It refuses when a shot render is older than its source and names the shot: render and stitch again. It encodes the stitched master frame for frame into `animatic.mp4` with `audio.track` and, for a film with `voice`, the narration from `voice.startFrame`, both cut to the film length (no ducking or loudness change; `mix` does those). It prints the animatic's liveness verdict as advice: present it at G3 beside the animatic, and send each still span to the builder of the shot it names while changes are cheap. The verdict never blocks G3; G4 enforces the limits. Present the animatic as blocking at placeholder fidelity, not finished motion. Without the CLI: stitch the blocking renders of the primary format as in step 2, then mux the track and the narration with ffmpeg (`adelay` the narration to its start, `amix=normalize=0`, `-t <film seconds>`). Completion: `animatic.mp4` plays from first to last frame, shows the current blocking renders, and carries the real track and the narration.
2. Render video-only clips per shot and format. Normalize, check exact counts, align cuts, compare declared handoffs, then stitch clips into one video-only master. Extract a 1-frame-per-second sheet and full-frame-rate transition strips. Verify the master duration and frame count with `ffprobe`. (CLI: `motion-studio render/stitch`, #2; `motion-studio handoff`, #3; `motion-studio sheet` after `stitch` writes the contact pages, the transition strips and their index `sheet.json` into `renders/<format>/`. With the CLI, follow its whole route render -> stitch -> handoff -> mix from `reference/v0-commands.md`; do not mix CLI steps with manual ones.) Completion: every master has the timeline's exact picture frames and no audio stream.
3. Mix the sole 48 kHz stereo master from track/voice and real SFX positioned by measured peaks; measure offsets and integrated loudness, normalize to -14 LUFS. Mux it onto each draft picture. Check held protected bounds for all format safe rectangles, and report unavailable safe-zone measurements. (CLI: `motion-studio mix`, #3, mixes and muxes every chosen format; `motion-studio safezone films/<slug>`, #9, over every shot; builders' `--shots` runs do not write the report.) `motion-studio mix` also mixes narration: it places the `voice.tts` audio at `voice.startFrame`, ducks `audio.track` 12 dB under the spoken phrases from `voice.wordTimings` (words less than 0.5 s apart share one phrase, 0.1 s linear ramps) and lists the ducked spans and each spoken reveal against its word frame in `sync.json`. After normalizing to -14 LUFS it limits true peaks to -1 dBTP and reports the largest gain reduction. It refuses a voice whose `tts` is `null`. Without the CLI, mix manually with the same ducking:
   - Phrases: convert each word to film seconds (`voice.startFrame / fps + start`, and the same for `end`). Merge words less than 0.5 s apart into one phrase `[a, b]`.
   - Duck the bed with a volume expression: for each phrase `d_i = clip((t-a+0.1)/0.1,0,1)*clip((b+0.1-t)/0.1,0,1)`, combine phrases with nested `max(d_1,max(d_2,...))` as `D`, and set the bed gain to `1-0.749*D` (0.749 = 1 - 10^(-12/20), so -12 dB inside a phrase and linear 0.1 s ramps on each side).
   - Sum at 48 kHz stereo, for example `ffmpeg -i <bed.wav> -i <voice.wav> -filter_complex "[0:a]aresample=48000,aformat=channel_layouts=stereo,asetnsamples=n=48,volume=eval=frame:volume='1-0.749*<D>'[b];[1:a]aresample=48000,aformat=channel_layouts=stereo,adelay=<start-ms>:all=1,apad[v];[b][v]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:normalize=0[m]" -map "[m]" -c:a pcm_s24le <mix.wav>`. `asetnsamples=n=48` makes the volume update every 1 ms. The bed sets the length; trim or pad it to the film first.
   - Add SFX by measured peaks, measure integrated LUFS with `loudnorm` `print_format=json` (see the `motion-critique` evidence packet), and apply one gain to reach -14 LUFS. Then hold true peaks at -1 dBTP, for example `alimiter=limit=0.85:attack=2:release=50:level=false` (0.85 is about -1.4 dBFS sample peak, which leaves room for the peaks between samples). Measure again: `-af ebur128=peak=true` must show integrated -14 +/- 0.5 LUFS and a true peak at or below -1 dBFS; if the loudness dropped, raise the gain and limit again. Mux onto the picture. Report a manual mix as manual. Completion: every draft has one audio mix, measured sync and a safe-zone report.
4. Run `motion-studio scan films/<slug>` after `stitch` and before dispatching `motion-critique`. Declare intended holds, effects and spring or bounce moves in `storyboard.json` (`holds`, `effects`, `moves`) rather than ignoring flags. Blocking flags (sudden blank dropouts, fully flat shots, frame-count errors, unintended pops) exit 1; a flat opening, ending or fade is an advisory `flat` flag; advisory flags go to the reviewer with `renders/<format>/scan.json`. For loop genres compare last/first image and sound seam (`motion-studio loopcheck`, not yet ticketed). Completion: technical blockers are fixed and ambiguous flags are attached to review.
5. Polish before G4. After the full build, run the polish checklist (`motion-direction` `reference/polish.md`) with each full-mode builder for its shots: motion blur on fast moves, easing and spring per the look, secondary motion, depth and light, texture, type finish, frame rate and a readable brand name at the end. Render and stitch again after the last change, then run step 4 again. Completion: every checklist item of every shot is done, or not needed with a written reason.
6. Run `motion-studio liveness films/<slug>` after every `stitch` and before presenting G4. It measures stillness in each master against the D59 limits, writes `renders/<format>/liveness.json` and lists each still span over 0.5 s with its shot and film frames. Fix a failing span with motion: a living hold keeps something moving. Then render, stitch and measure again. G4 approval is refused while a report is missing, stale or failing; only the director waives it (`gate films/<slug> G4 approve --waive liveness --note "<reason>"`). Give the report to the reviewer with the scan. Completion: every chosen format has a current passing report, or the director's waiver is recorded.
7. Dispatch a fresh reviewer before G4 (critique loop A, D78). After `sheet`, `scan`, `mix` and `liveness` on the final stitched master, run `motion-studio packet films/<slug> [format]` for every chosen format and dispatch a fresh `motion-critique` reviewer with the packet paths (`motion-critique` step 2). The reviewer checks the film; it does not direct it: you stay director and builder, and you choose which findings to repair. `gate films/<slug> G4 approve` refuses unless `critique/loop-<n>.md` is an independent report whose header `Packet:` line names each format's current packet and master SHA-256. With no subagent tool, the inline review is non-independent: approve with `--waive critique --note "<reason>"` (D80), which still needs the current packet and report. After a repair, render, stitch, measure and run `packet` again, and dispatch a new fresh reviewer. Completion: each chosen format has a critique report of its current master, independent or covered by the critique waiver, and the report is presented at G4.
8. Before G5, present the poster, sheet, safe-zone report, unresolved rights and expected final-render cost. Await approval of cost, then render final per-format MP4s, poster, contact sheet, loop report where needed, ledger and a source README. Show files for user acceptance. Completion: bundle files are directly probed, rights decisions are explicit and accepted files match approved inputs.
