# Render and delivery phase

Inputs: approved storyboard and frozen stills, shot projects, layouts, ledger, beat grid, audio source files and current gate hashes. Output: `animatic.mp4`, per-format draft and final bundles under `renders/<format>/`, mix and reports.

1. For G3, hold each **entry and last in-range still** for the shot's allocated frames and add the real track. Mark this as an animatic, not completed motion. Inspect duration and transitions before showing it. (CLI: `motion-studio animatic`, #7.) Completion: G3 plays from first to last frame and uses the actual audio source.
2. Render video-only clips per shot and format. Normalize, check exact counts, align cuts, compare declared handoffs, then stitch clips into one video-only master. Extract a 1-frame-per-second sheet and full-frame-rate transition strips. Verify the master duration and frame count with `ffprobe`. (CLI: `motion-studio render/stitch`, #2; `motion-studio handoff`, #3; `motion-studio sheet`, #7.) Completion: every master has the timeline's exact picture frames and no audio stream.
3. Mix the sole 48 kHz stereo master from track/voice and real SFX positioned by measured peaks; measure offsets and integrated loudness, normalize to -14 LUFS. Mux it onto each draft picture. Check held protected bounds for all format safe rectangles, and report unavailable safe-zone measurements. (CLI: `motion-studio mix`, #3, muxes the primary format only, so mux the other formats with ffmpeg; `motion-studio safezone`, #9.) `motion-studio mix` does not mix narration yet: it reads `audio.track` and SFX cues and ignores `voice.tts` (voice support: #19; #22 makes `mix` reject a film with voice). For a film with voice, mix manually: place the voice at its start offset, duck the bed under it, and sum at 48 kHz stereo, for example `ffmpeg -i <bed.wav> -i <voice.wav> -filter_complex "[1:a]aresample=48000,aformat=channel_layouts=stereo,adelay=<start-ms>:all=1,apad,asplit[v1][v2];[0:a]aresample=48000,aformat=channel_layouts=stereo[b];[b][v1]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.05:ratio=6:release=250[duck];[duck][v2]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:normalize=0[m]" -map "[m]" -c:a pcm_s24le <mix.wav>`. The bed sets the length; trim or pad it to the film first. Add SFX by measured peaks, measure integrated LUFS with `loudnorm` `print_format=json` (see the `motion-critique` evidence packet), apply one gain to reach -14 LUFS, measure again, then mux onto the picture. Report this mix as manual. Completion: every draft has one audio mix, measured sync and a safe-zone report.
4. Run `motion-studio scan films/<slug>` after `stitch` and before dispatching `motion-critique`. Declare intended holds and effects in `storyboard.json` (`holds`, `effects`) rather than ignoring flags. Blocking flags (blank frames, frame-count errors, unintended pops) exit 1; advisory flags go to the reviewer with `renders/<format>/scan.json`. For loop genres compare last/first image and sound seam (`motion-studio loopcheck`, not yet ticketed). Completion: technical blockers are fixed and ambiguous flags are attached to review.
5. Before G5, present the poster, sheet, safe-zone report, unresolved rights and expected final-render cost. Await approval of cost, then render final per-format MP4s, poster, contact sheet, loop report where needed, ledger and a source README. Show files for user acceptance. Completion: bundle files are directly probed, rights decisions are explicit and accepted files match approved inputs.
